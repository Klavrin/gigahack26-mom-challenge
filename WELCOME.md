# Welcome to the Future of Meeting Minutes 🚀

First time here? You have landed on a project built to solve a real problem:
turning multilingual hospital meetings into clear, useful, and actionable minutes—without
sending sensitive conversations to the cloud.

## Why this project is awesome

- **Privacy comes first.** The entire pipeline can run on the hospital's own network.
- **It understands real conversations.** Romanian, Russian, and English can appear in the
  same meeting, even alongside specialized medical vocabulary.
- **It creates more than a transcript.** The system extracts decisions, action items,
  owners, and deadlines so teams know exactly what happens next.
- **Humans stay in control.** Minutes can be reviewed and edited before they are approved
  and delivered.
- **It works beyond the demo.** The project includes a browser interface, local AI models,
  workflow automation, email delivery, evaluation tools, and offline deployment support.

## The 30-second story

Record or upload a meeting. The project separates speech into useful chunks, recognizes
the language being spoken, transcribes the conversation, structures the important details,
and prepares professional minutes for review and delivery.

All of that happens locally. That is especially important in healthcare, where privacy is
not an optional feature—it is part of the product.
