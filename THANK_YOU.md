# Thank You for Believing in Our Idea 💙

Thank you for taking the time to explore our project.

Every visit, conversation, question, and piece of feedback helps us understand where this
idea can create the most value. Your interest is more than encouragement—it helps validate
that secure, practical, and human-centered AI has an important place in healthcare.

## Why your support matters

We built this project around a simple belief: healthcare teams should spend less time
writing meeting notes and more time acting on what matters, without compromising the
privacy of sensitive conversations.

When you test the project, share your perspective, or tell us which problems matter most,
you help us:

- validate the real-world need for private, on-premise meeting intelligence;
- improve the experience for medical and administrative teams;
- identify the features that would make the biggest practical difference;
- demonstrate that multilingual AI can be both useful and responsible; and
- turn a hackathon idea into something with lasting impact.

## To everyone supporting us

To the people reviewing our work, offering technical advice, sharing domain knowledge,
testing the demo, or simply cheering us on: **thank you**.

Your support gives us the confidence to keep refining the idea. Your honest feedback makes
the project stronger. Your validation reminds us that solving meaningful problems is always
a team effort.

## Help us move the idea forward

If this project resonates with you, you can support it by:

- trying the demo and telling us about your experience;
- sharing the project with someone who understands healthcare workflows;
- suggesting real meeting scenarios we should support;
- opening an issue with an idea or improvement; or
- giving the repository a star to help more people discover it.

Thank you for checking out our vision and being part of its journey. We are grateful to
have you here—and excited about what we can build next. 🚀
